*"Friends don't lie."* — Eleven


*Stranger Things* is a sci-fi horror drama television series created by the Duffer Brothers for Netflix. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of Hawkins, Indiana, it follows a group of kids as they uncover supernatural mysteries.

## ✨ Why I Love It
* *The 80s Nostalgia:* The music, fashion, and movie references are incredible.
* *The Characters:* The bond between Eleven, Mike, Dustin, Lucas, and Will feels genuine.
* *The Upside Down:* The spooky, alternate dimension adds high-stakes thrills.
